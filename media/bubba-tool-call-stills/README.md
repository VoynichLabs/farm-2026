# Bubba stills from the Tool Call music video

Every picture of Bubba generated for the VoynichLabs "Tool Call" music video (5 Oct 2026), kept for future videos and art.
They were made with Google's Nano Banana 2 image model through OpenRouter.

- `_contact-sheet.jpg` shows all of them at once.
- The first-wave pictures (`01_hero` to `12_loaded`) are the plain handyman and the first cyber looks.
- `11_hero_cyber_b` and `12_loaded_c` are the reference pictures used to keep his face, hard hat and BUBBA patch the same; pass one of them as a reference image when generating new Bubba pictures.
- `prompts-stills.py` holds the exact prompts and the shared description of his look.

Source copy: music-videos repo, `toolcall-video/out/stills/`. Backup: Samsung SSD, `data/music-video/tool-call/stills/`.
