# Birdcatraz Pen — Physical Specs

**Logged:** 03-Sep-2026, from Boss's product listing (Discord #meet-the-lobsters)

## Producer's Pride 8x8 ft Universal Poultry Pen

- Sturdy steel frame, welded-wire construction, powder-coat finish (rust resistant)
- Welded-wire roof
- Large access door, 2-way heavy-duty locking latch, padlock-compatible
- Bottom panel spacing: 1.83 in x 1.96 in (46.5 mm x 50 mm) — predator-resistant
- Removable wire panel for customization
- Panel-clamp assembly; connects to other pens to expand (sold separately)

**Overall dimensions:** 8 ft W x 8.4 ft D x 6.7 ft H (7.43 m W x 2.57 m D x 2.05 m H)

## Tarp coverage

- **Top:** already covered — existing 10x10 ft pink tarp over the welded-wire roof.
- **Sides:** two of the four walls still need covering (see sizing below).

## Tarp sizing — covering two adjacent sides (wraparound at one corner)

- Combined wall length: 8 ft + 8.4 ft = 16.4 ft
- Wall height: 6.7 ft
- **Recommendation: one 20 ft x 10 ft poly tarp**, wrapped around the corner and tied off.
  - 20 ft length covers the 16.4 ft combined run with ~3.6 ft slack for overlap/tie-down at the ends
  - 10 ft height covers the 6.7 ft wall with ~3.3 ft slack to overhang the top rail and stake down at ground level
  - Single continuous tarp = fewer seams, better wind/rain seal at the corner than two separate tarps
- Alternative if two separate tarps preferred: two 10 ft x 8 ft tarps, one per side (each has slack on both dimensions).
