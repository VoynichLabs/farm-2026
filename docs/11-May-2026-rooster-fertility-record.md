## Poultry fertility assumptions

- Eggs can be fertile as soon as ~1 day after mating.
- In small flocks, good fertility is expected within ~4 days after introducing/isolating vigorous males.
- After a rooster is removed, fertility usually begins declining after ~4–5 days and may remain satisfactory for about a week.
- Hens can store sperm long enough for fertile eggs to appear for 3+ weeks after a rooster is removed.
- Therefore, exact paternity is probabilistic unless hens were isolated for ~21 days after the previous rooster was removed.
