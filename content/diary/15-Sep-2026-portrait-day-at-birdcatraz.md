# Portrait Day at Birdcatraz — 2026-09-15

The cameras caught an unusually photogenic flock today — **s7-cam logged reacted shots of ten different birds between 11:42 and 13:58**, nearly all looking straight into the lens: white-and-black and red-comb roosters, a rust-and-gold hen, a black-and-gold rooster, a speckled hen at the water bowl. Activity carried on steadily through evening, with roosters standing alert at the waterer and pecking the yard as late as 19:02.

The picture-taking had a purpose: with band checks in hand, three birds got fresh solo portraits confirmed today — **Birdsilla** (white band #3), **Henriessa** (pink band #8), and **Birdthazar** (purple band, faded number), the latter finally photographed solo again after months as a background bird.

The week's sadder note was resolved: footage review traced **Birddor's death to bird-on-bird violence**, not a predator — he'd been warned off the roof and didn't come down.
