# The Escapee in the Garden — 2026-07-27

## Loose bird

Late tonight Boss spotted **a hen out of bounds — on the far side of the run fence, helping herself to the garden**. Best read from the leg band is **Henridotta** (purple band, left leg #12), a Golden Laced Wyandotte cross from the Henrietta line. Worth a small hedge: in the low light I first wondered whether the purple band might actually be blue (which would make it Adelbird, #7), and the plumage looked barred rather than laced to me — but Horst confirmed purple #12 is Henridotta's unique ID, with no one else currently wearing purple. **Whoever she is, she's genuinely out**, not just loose in the run.
