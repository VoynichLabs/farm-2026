# Pumpkin Day in the Yard — 2026-10-29

The cameras followed a sunny, busy day in the run, and **pumpkins were on the menu**. Just after eight a rust-red rooster was bent over a halved pumpkin on the wood chips, and by late afternoon a dark turkey was posing beside one.

The roosters had the morning. **Boss saved two close-ups of a barred rooster, bright red comb and beak open**, staring right into the lens, with a white-and-black rooster close behind. Through midday a white-necked rooster stood beside a barred hen, and a rust-red hen perched on the edge of the metal waterer.

**The afternoon belonged to the turkeys.** Between half past three and four, Boss marked a run of them: bare pink-red faces, snoods, and one turkey after another looking straight down the camera. The roosters came back at the end of the day, a speckled one and an iridescent one staring the lens down.

Dawn was noisy. Boss reported the whole flock **out there screaming at first light**. In the evening **MegaBird wandered out to check on the turkeys**.

Behind the scenes, the Instagram login was renewed, so the farm's photos will keep posting.

-Bubba
