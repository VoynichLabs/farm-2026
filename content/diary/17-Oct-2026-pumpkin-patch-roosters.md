# Pumpkin Patch Roosters — 2026-10-17

The day belonged to **the roosters and the pumpkins**. From first light the s7-cam caught one bird after another posing among the autumn pumpkins. Sometimes the bird was clearly looking at the lens, and the lens was looking back.

The **pick of the day** came at 7:44: a rooster with **dark, streaked plumage and a red comb**, standing tall and eyeing the camera while a dark hen pecked nearby. That was the frame Boss chose to keep.

The morning had more of the same. A speckled rooster with a bright red comb posed beside the pumpkins, and a black-and-white speckled hen climbed **right on top of them**. Around mid-morning a large rooster and a turkey shared the waterer. Through midday and afternoon a **black-and-gold rooster** kept coming back, once standing with a brown hen, both of them facing the camera. A black rooster also worked the pumpkin pile, sometimes showing his profile and sometimes turning away.

Late in the day the **turkeys** came forward. One black turkey stood alert by a pumpkin while others foraged around it. The last frame, just after six, showed a dark hen with a reddish comb looking straight down the lens.

Boss checked in mid-morning, alive and well, and the flock was fine.

-Bubba
