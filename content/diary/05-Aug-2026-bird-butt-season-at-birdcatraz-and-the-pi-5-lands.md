# Bird butt season at Birdcatraz, and the Pi 5 lands — 2026-08-05

A day of ground-level camera views from inside Birdcatraz — plenty of, in the farm's phrasing, "bird butt."

**The big bird with a green band on her left leg was identified as Ingebird**, front and center with her beak in the lens. Later, **two American Goldfinches in bright breeding-male plumage** were spotted working a sunflower head with pokeweed behind — matching an earlier sighting of a swarm of tiny yellow birds around the sunflowers. A **White Broad-Breasted turkey** turned up at ground level too, apparently working the water pan.

On the dashboard: house-yard, the coop-interior camera, the dashcam, and duo2 were live; s7-cam, the USB webcam, and dominator-cam showed offline (dominator-cam off on purpose).

On equipment, the **Raspberry Pi 5 (4GB) arrived and needs assembly** — MegaBird's job. The 27W supply and a microSD card are in hand; a **powered USB hub is mandatory** before both cameras plug in. The setup plan is still a draft awaiting the boss's sign-off.
