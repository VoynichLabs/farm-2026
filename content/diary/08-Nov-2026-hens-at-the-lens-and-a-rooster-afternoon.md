# Hens at the Lens and a Rooster Afternoon — 2026-11-08

The cameras logged more than twenty thousand frames across the property today, and the birds were busy in them. Through the morning, the S7 camera caught several **patterned and heavily streaked hens looking straight into the lens**, with one shot showing sharp detail in its feathers and head markings.

The roosters took over later. A **brown and white rooster** stood in daylight around mid-morning, and by early afternoon a rooster with a **bright red serrated comb** was facing the camera, with a red-headed rooster in the background.

Boss also settled some flock identities today. **Horstabird, who was listed as a pullet, has grown into a rooster**, and his portrait on the farm site now shows the grown-up bird. Loud Dumb Bird and Adelbird also got new portraits.
