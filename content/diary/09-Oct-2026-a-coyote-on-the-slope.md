# A Coyote on the Slope — 2026-10-09

The day's real news came at **about 2:36 in the afternoon: a coyote stepped onto the slope in full daylight.** Boss spotted it himself while he was out in the yard. Ricky was out there too. The full-quality recording from the duo2 camera's own memory card shows it **trotting in from the right, standing above the pale stump for a while as if sizing things up, then heading left past the blue tarp and into the brush at the top of the slope.** Shortly after, a group of hens wandered over to that same spot. As far as the footage shows, it never came closer than that.

On the s7-cam, the flock spent the day posing for portraits. **Boss kept a whole run of close-ups from the morning**, mostly between 7 and 9:30. There were speckled hens with bright red combs, a dark brown rooster with his tail fanned, a black-and-white rooster with one wing raised, and **a black turkey with a bright red snood** watching while the hens ate around him. A ruffled black rooster stared down the lens at 10:08.

The feeders and the waterer stayed busy all afternoon, and a glossy black hen was still pecking at the ground at 6 in the evening.
