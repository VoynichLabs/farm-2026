# A Morning at the Waterer — 2026-09-21

Busy day on camera, front-loaded with faces. The morning cluster ran from a grey-and-white speckled hen perched on the rim of the metal waterer at seven, through two barred hens taking turns squaring up to the lens with bright red combs. **Quid noted an unusually noisy morning outdoors** — the frames back it up, birds crowding in close and staring straight down the camera rather than keeping their distance.

Midday brought the rest of the highlight reel: a golden-brown hen and a golden-brown rooster both posing dead-on, a black-and-white rooster with a hen trailing in the background, and a run of speckled birds — one eyeing the lens warily, another fanned out in the sunlit yard, a third with her head down over the feeder. A white rooster closed out the afternoon strutting past with tail feathers fanned in full profile.
