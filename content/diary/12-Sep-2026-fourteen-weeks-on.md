# Fourteen Weeks On — 2026-09-12

The pen ran bright and busy all day. **Seven frames earned a human's mark**, nearly all of them close portraits with the eye in sharp focus: a speckled grey-and-white hen staring down the lens at midday with rust and black birds massed behind her, a **copper-and-gold rooster in full profile** at 13:46, and a grey hen with a big fluffy white crest bending to drink at 14:27. The barred black-and-white hens owned the afternoon — reacted to four separate times between 13:34 and 18:19, twice standing at the waterer.

Around the edges, an ordinary good day: a dark brown rooster beside a white hen at first light, a white rooster near the red-rimmed waterer at eleven, greens picked over at 15:06 and again by a copper-red rooster at dusk. **19,375 frames captured farm-wide.**

The day's real news came off a clear group shot: **all six junior Ornitharchs from June's final clutch sitting together** — Birdimir, Ingebird, Horstabird, Henriessa, Henridotta and Adelbird, hatched 2–4 June, now **right around 14 weeks old**. Identifying each by coloration is still open.
