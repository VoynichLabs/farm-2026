# Pumpkins and Doorstep Turkeys — 2026-10-24

The roosters owned the camera today. Boss kept frame after frame of them **staring straight into the lens**: a black-and-gold rooster at breakfast, a red-combed one mid-morning, a dark streaked rooster with his tail cocked after lunch, and a black rooster near dusk. The hens got their turn too. A black-and-white speckled hen bowed over a bowl, and a rust-and-black hen gave the lens a long look.

**Malt Liquor and Hawk Food started the day**, crowing well before sunrise. Boss knows their voices.

**Pumpkins were the afternoon's entertainment.** A golden rooster foraged beside one, and turkeys kept coming back to peck at them, with one barred turkey working the ground among them.

Around midday the whole flock got loud. The cause turned out to be **turkeys on the doorstep**: two big white ones staring in like they were owed rent, with hens poking around the trash cans and the Yorkie calm beside the bin. Later, two white toms and a dark turkey settled into the wood chips, **one fanning his tail**.

The Birdcatraz Pi came unplugged and still hadn't come back online by evening. It's coming indoors to be checked.
