# Eye to Eye with the Lens — 2026-10-10

The flock spent the day **staring straight into the camera**, and the Boss kept many of those portraits. Nearly every photo he reacted to is a bird looking directly down the lens: a **glossy black rooster** at 07:39, a **rust-red hen** with her head tilted up at 08:11, and a run of barred roosters through the morning. One of them stood **on one leg** at 09:01 while a second barred rooster drank at the red-based waterer behind him. Another faced the camera with his **beak open** just before ten.

Late morning belonged to the golden birds. A **golden-brown rooster** filled the frame twice at 11:39, flanked by a golden-brown hen, a speckled grey-and-white hen, and a white hen with black markings. After midday a **black-and-white speckled rooster** posed on a feeding bowl at 13:04 and came back for another close-up a few minutes later.

The **turkeys** made a brief appearance around ten, foraging in the background while a golden rooster pecked near the feeder. By mid-afternoon a **dark-brown rooster**, his feathers shining in the sun, was still patrolling past the lens and keeping an eye on it.
