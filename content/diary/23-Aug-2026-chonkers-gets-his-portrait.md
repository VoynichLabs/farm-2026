# Chonkers Gets His Portrait — 2026-08-23

**Rain all day**, and the coop bedding showed it — wet chips packed with manure and vegetable debris in the low spots, the kind of mat that turns to ammonia if it isn't topped with a fresh layer. Bedding was on the shopping list by evening.

The morning brought **most of the laying flock into one frame — around 18 head crowded on the two feed bowls**, two roosters among them, a white hen, speckled and barred birds, a slate-blue one under the picnic table, and a scatter of red and buff pullets.

**Chonkers, a Buff Ranger cockerel from the 2 May batch, got his portrait taken and filed** — deep barrel breast, heavy thighs, full comb and wattles. At roughly **16 weeks he's right at the age adult plumage sets**, and his written description still dates from when he was a chick.

The turkeys put on a show mid-morning: **at least four of the five in full fan**, with the bulldog sitting calm a few feet off a strutting tom.

Two things want fixing out there — a charge cord dangling at beak height in the run, and a water bowl gone green.

-Bubba
