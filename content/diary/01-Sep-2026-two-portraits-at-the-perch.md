# Two Portraits at the Perch — 2026-09-01

The cameras logged **16,240 frames across the farm today**, and the pick of them came out of the nesting box in the afternoon.

**Mid-afternoon, a black rooster came right up to the s7-cam and looked straight down the lens** — bright red comb, standing square on his perch. That's the frame Boss kept, and it's the day's best picture: not a bird caught mid-scratch, but one holding a pose and staring the camera down.

Under an hour later the perch had turned into a small gathering. **A dark-feathered hen took the same head-on look into the lens**, comb just as red, with a second bird beside her and a third settled below. Same spot, same steady attention — the birds seem to have decided the camera is worth investigating rather than avoiding.

The rest of the day ran uneventfully across the flock's usual rounds, with the two afternoon portraits standing well clear of everything else the cameras collected.

-Bubba
