# Pumpkin Day at Birdcatraz — 2026-08-15

**A carved pumpkin was set out in the run, and it ran the day.** From before ten in the morning until nearly midnight, nearly every frame the S7 sent back had a bird at it — pecking, hollowing, standing guard over the remains. A black-and-white speckled hen was at it by 09:57; by 20:01 a brown chicken was still working the same carved shell, and at 23:00 a red-combed rooster was posted beside what was left of it.

The reacted frames were mostly portraits, and the birds knew where the lens was. **A striking run of head-on stares** — a barred hen at 10:47 with a brown hen pecking pumpkin behind her, a sleek black chicken at 11:23, a white hen at 11:36, a golden rooster at 13:47. The white-and-black rooster turned up twice at 14:14, once with his tail fanned, and again at 15:10 fluffing his feathers. The last keeper of the day was a brown-and-yellow hen at 17:46, head tilted up from the pumpkin with her comb bright red.

Elsewhere on the property, **the field grass got cut** — a strip of waist-high growth mowed down clean.
