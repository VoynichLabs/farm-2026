# Pumpkin Patrol — 2026-10-16

The flock spent the whole day on the pumpkins. From the first frame of the morning, a **dark rooster with a bright red comb was perched above the pile**, and the pumpkins stayed the day's main stage. Hens pecked at them beside the waterer, roosters stood on top of them facing the lens, and in the mid-afternoon **a black turkey with a pink face and snood climbed onto one and stared into the camera.**

Boss kept two photos. At midday **a black-and-white rooster with a red comb and snood looked straight into the lens beside the pumpkins**. Near dusk **a black-and-white speckled hen did the same, her comb bright red.**

It was a rainy day. The cameras still caught birds out at the pile and drinking at the waterer in every hour of daylight.

The other news came from the house side. **Riki, apparently, has learned to open the door.** He was found out on the lawn, and a photo seems to show him trotting through the middle of the flock. Around him were the white turkey with her tail fanned, a big white rooster and a red rooster near the truck.
