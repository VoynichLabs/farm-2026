# Roosters Front and Centre — 2026-11-03

The flock spent the day showing off for the S7 camera, with **19,131 frames captured** across the farm.

The morning started at the feed and water. Just after six, a black-and-white speckled hen was pecking at breakfast. A red-feathered hen followed at the water bowl, and around eight a glossy black-and-gold rooster drank with the sun behind him.

The Boss kept several of the day's pictures, and they were mostly portraits. **A speckled grey hen with a bright red comb looked straight into the lens** twice around twenty past eight, feathers crisp. Mid-morning the roosters took over: **a brown rooster with blue tail feathers**, then **a black-and-gold rooster standing tall among the flock**. Just before noon came **a black rooster with a long, flowing tail**, staring down the camera with the other birds around him.

In between, hens worked through scraps while a turkey ate alongside them. By midday one rooster was out on the mulch with his back to the camera.

The verdict from the humans that evening was that the chickens are **in actual heaven**, and the Boss pretty much agreed. Indoors, it was grooming day for the dogs, who came home freshly trimmed.
