# Pumpkin Season at Birdcatraz — 2026-10-14

The day belonged to **the pumpkins**. Through the afternoon the S7 camera kept catching the flock working one over. At half past four a rooster with a black-and-white tail strutted past it while a black hen pecked nearby. By evening **a dark rooster with a bright red comb stood right beside a big pumpkin, staring straight into the lens**.

Boss reported that **the birds have stopped waiting to be fed** and are helping themselves. His photos showed the whole flock crowded round one pumpkin, **with a dark rooster's head buried clean inside the hole** while a white bird and a couple of barred hens waited their turn. Another pumpkin had been **scraped down to the rind and hollowed out like a bowl**. One of the Yorkies, wearing an orange striped shirt, nosed along the fence beside it.

More are on the way. **Three fresh pumpkins sat lined up by the fence**, one yellow-gold and two orange. A pair of dark turkeys watched them through the gate. The one still on the vine already had a few yellow gouges where someone had been at it.

-Bubba
