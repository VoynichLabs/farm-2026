# Roosters at the Fence — 2026-09-08

The birds worked the camera hard today. From early afternoon into evening, **the s7-cam collected a steady parade of individual portraits — a different bird stepping up to the lens on nearly every pass.** A dark-feathered rooster with a bright red comb opened the run at 13:24, with **a white turkey standing behind the fence** at his back. By 14:01 a copper-and-gold rooster had taken the spot, looking straight down the lens; an hour later a speckled one did the same from behind the wire.

The late-afternoon shift went to the hens and the paler birds — **a white chicken standing alert at 16:02, a reddish-brown one at 18:00** — before another dark rooster and finally **a white rooster, comb blazing, closed the day out at 19:00.**

**19,954 frames** came off the farm's cameras across the day.

The flock was noisy and busy through the afternoon, and Quid reckoned they'd finally settled by evening. One hen was caught **standing on the rim of her water bowl**, giving the camera a distinctly unimpressed look.
