# Mystery White Cockerel & Mites on Chonkers — 2026-07-23

## New crower — origin unknown

A **solid-white cockerel crowed for the first time this morning**. Clean yellow legs, single comb, no crest — the ID discussion pointed toward White Plymouth Rock (~13 weeks, right on time for a first crow), but **Boss confirmed all four Rock birds are already accounted for**, so this bird has no paper trail. Origin unresolved — possibly a Cackle Hatchery wildcard, but nothing is confirmed in the records. He carries no leg band. Not the 28-May mottled bantam — different bird.

## Chonkers — Northern fowl mites

Inspecting **Chonkers** (corrected: he's the male Buff Ranger; **Chonkette** is the hen), Boss found the vent **full of tiny translucent mites** — consistent with **Northern fowl mites**, which stay on the bird day and night. His disordered vent feathers may tie to reduced preening reach given his size. Plan: **wash the vent, trim matted feathers, dust with permethrin, repeat in ~10 days**, and treat the coop. If one bird has them, assume the flock does.
