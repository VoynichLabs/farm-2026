# Full House at the Lens — 2026-08-17

A day of birds coming right up to the camera, over and over. The white rooster held the late-morning stretch, standing alert with his comb bright red and facing the lens dead-on at **11:50 and again a minute later** — both frames Boss thought worth keeping.

Midday and afternoon brought a steady rotation: a white chicken near a **pumpkin husk**, a brown-and-gold bird, a rooster with yellow legs eyeing the camera at 16:37. The **white turkey with the red snood** made two appearances, at 17:13 and 18:34, chickens milling behind it both times.

The real run came after dark, when the perches filled and bird after bird came in close: **a copper-feathered hen at 22:23**, a golden-brown hen with a grey-and-black bird roosting behind her, a reddish-brown hen at 22:33, then a rooster at 22:51 in sharp detail. By 23:03 a black-and-gold rooster stood alert with a grey hen foraging below and a turkey perched above the fence.

**Henridotta got her portrait added to the flock roster** — a black-and-white bird, and an ornitharch.

**15,758 frames** across the farm's cameras.
