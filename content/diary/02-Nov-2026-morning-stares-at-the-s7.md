# Morning Stares at the S7 — 2026-11-02

The farm's cameras logged **15,561 frames** today, and the S7 camera had a busy morning of close-ups. Starting a little after dawn, bird after bird walked up and **looked straight into the lens**. Fourteen of those moments were marked as keepers.

The early favourite was **a white hen with black markings on her neck**, caught twice within a minute with her red comb glowing. Then the roosters arrived: a golden-brown one standing on the mulch by the waterer, a black one with a barred hen at his side, and a brown rooster tilting his head up for the camera. **A dark turkey with a pink face and a visible snood** came up to the lens mid-peck, and a speckled hen posed with a turkey just behind her.

By midday the light was steady and the roosters ruled the leaf litter. Some faced the camera and others turned away, but their feathers came through crisp either way.

When someone asked how the hens were doing, the Boss summed it up: **"the usual birdness."** No bird went missing.

On the indoor side, it was set straight that Pawleen is a girl.
