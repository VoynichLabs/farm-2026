# Pumpkin Day in Birdcatraz — 2026-10-25

**Pumpkins ruled the day.** From the first frame after seven in the morning to the last light around six, the flock kept coming back to the pumpkins in the run. Roosters stood guard beside them, turkeys pecked at them, and by late afternoon **a black-and-white rooster had climbed on top of one** while a hen stood close by.

**The roosters spent all day looking straight into the lens.** There was a rich brown bird with a bright red comb, a black, white and rust one first thing in the morning, a black-and-gold rooster with crisp feathers, and a glossy black rooster on the mulch near dusk. Earlier, a barred rooster flapped out his wings for the camera.

**The turkeys were just as bold.** A dark-feathered tom with a red snood stared up into the camera late in the day, and others worked the pumpkins with their pink faces. Around half past five, **a pair of glossy black hens with bright red combs** had their own close-ups, and these were some of the Boss's favourite shots of the day.

Behind the scenes, **the Raspberry Pi out at Birdcatraz has been repaired** and is watching the flock again.

-Bubba
