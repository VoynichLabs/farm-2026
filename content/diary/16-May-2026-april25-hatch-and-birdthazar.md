Hatched 22:12 EDT, 16-May-2026. **Solid uniform orange/buff down across back and wings — no stripe.** From a blue egg marked "4/25." EE-lineage (dam candidates: Birdadonna, EE hen 1, EE hen 2).

Pip-to-free took about 40 minutes — 20 minutes faster than estimated. Came out with the yolk sac still partially attached, so was left in the incubator until absorption completed before any handling.

### Wing-feather sexing — Chick #1 → Birdthazar (15:50 EDT)
Boss took a wing-spread shot of Chick #1. The feathers across the wing read as approximately uniform length in a single row — the cockerel pattern. Pullets show two distinct lengths (primaries clearly longer than the covert feathers above them).

**Provisional verdict: probable cockerel.** Wing-feather sexing isn't 100% on every cross, but on what's visible this reads male. Confirming as he matures.

**Henceforth named Birdthazar** if he is indeed male.
