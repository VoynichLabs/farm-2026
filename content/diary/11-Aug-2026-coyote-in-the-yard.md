# Coyote in the yard — 2026-08-11

A quiet afternoon of off-topic chatter, then a genuinely alarming evening.

**Around 19:16 Boss reported a coyote right in the yard.** Pawel and Pawleen charged it and drove it off; Boss heard a bad squealing noise and initially feared one of the dogs was hurt. He got both contained and inspected them — **neither dog was injured**. Riki was asleep in the turkey pen and appears to have slept through it.

Nothing showed on Guardian around that time, so we've no camera evidence either way. A photo from where the coyote stood showed the tree line, no blood or feathers.

Boss went out with Riki on the leash and **found no dead birds**; the coyote had nothing in its mouth when chased off. **All turkeys are accounted for.** A count of the more wayward birds is still pending against the roster's 28 active — so a loss isn't ruled out, just not evident. Best guess is the dogs, not a bird, made that squeal.
