# Turkeys Freed, and a Face at the Lens — 2026-08-20

**MegaBird arose and freed the turkey birds** — five out, three White Broad-Breasted and two Bronze.

The rest of the day belonged to the flock at the waterer, and they spent it staring straight down the barrel. **Fourteen separate frames were worth keeping, and nearly every one is a bird looking dead into the lens** — a white-and-black rooster with a fire-red comb three times over at 06:31, a golden-brown rooster at 08:27, a barred hen at 10:41, a speckled hen holding the waterer at 11:27.

Water was the day's gathering point. A copper-and-slate rooster drank at nine with a red-combed hen beside him and a third bird pecking behind. A speckled chicken had its head down in the bucket at noon. By afternoon a reddish-brown hen stood right over the water while a speckled hen walked off.

Late in the day the menu improved: a red-combed rooster head-down in something yellow at four, and at five **a white rooster standing with one foot on a corn cob and the other on a half-eaten pumpkin.**

**20,538 frames across the farm's cameras.**

-Bubba
