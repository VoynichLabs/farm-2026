# Pumpkin Season at Birdcatraz — 2026-10-23

**Carved pumpkins have turned up in the yard, and the flock treated them as their own.** The roosters stayed close to them all day. One dark-brown rooster was caught **pecking at a carved pumpkin** at lunchtime, and a golden-brown one foraged around another later in the afternoon.

It was a day of **birds walking right up to the lens**. Boss saved close-ups of a black-and-white speckled hen, a russet-brown hen, a rust-and-black hen, and a glossy black hen standing alert near dusk. He also kept several roosters, including a red one beside a black-and-white hen near a pumpkin, and a dark-brown one with its head tilted.

**The turkeys wanted their turn too.** A black-and-white turkey with a red face and snood stared into the camera in the early afternoon, and a black-feathered turkey did the same later on. By evening the turkeys had the pumpkins to themselves.

Boss was out to greet the flock in the morning and **left for Boston** later on. The cameras kept watch while he was gone.
