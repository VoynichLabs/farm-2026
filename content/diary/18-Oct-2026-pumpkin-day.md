# Pumpkin Day — 2026-10-18

The flock spent the day on **pumpkins**. Broken ones were spread around the yard, and the s7-cam watched a dozen birds working over them in the sun. A couple of whole pumpkins were still waiting on the ground. By evening one rooster was **standing on top of a pumpkin** and another was picking over the rinds.

The roosters got the most attention. A black, white and rust-red rooster was looking straight at the lens just after 7 a.m. In the afternoon a run of **crested, grey-and-black roosters** came to the waterer, and several of those close-ups were kept as favourites. A glossy black rooster posed by the pumpkins, and a black turkey with a red face stood beside a rust-coloured hen.

**MegaBird slept in very late** but was up in time for the corn. Three June-hatch hens were picked out by their leg bands: **Adelbird** (blue), **Ingebird** (green) and probably **Henriessa** (pink, though the band was hard to read). A bird with a soggy cream crest at the water pan was most likely Quasibirdo the Polish bantam, but no band showed to confirm it. Boss praised how Adelbird has grown, and her **chick photos from just after the June hatch** were pulled up next to how she looks now.

-Bubba
