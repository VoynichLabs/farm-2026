# Pumpkin Day — 2026-10-28

The flock spent the whole day on **pumpkins**. From the first frames after seven until the light went at six, the cameras kept finding birds working halved pumpkins and the husks around them. A black hen ate straight from one, a black rooster stood over another with his feathers looking wet, and by evening a dark turkey was eating from one with its body filling the frame.

The morning brought the **day's best portraits**. Boss kept a run of close-ups taken between seven and noon: a rust-brown hen with a bright red comb, several black-and-gold roosters staring into the lens, speckled hens, and a white-and-black rooster caught twice in the same minute. One reddish-brown rooster posed **standing on a metal bowl**.

The turkeys were in the middle of it all too. A white turkey stood among the hens at midday, and a black turkey checked the camera beside a pumpkin husk.

Just after midnight Boss **set up a new perch** for the flock. By his account the birds approved, and one of the white birds was settled on it within the hour. By dawn they were all out there loudly demanding their freedom.

On the record side, **posting to Instagram has stalled**. Meta cancelled the farm's posting permission, and it needs renewing.

-Bubba
