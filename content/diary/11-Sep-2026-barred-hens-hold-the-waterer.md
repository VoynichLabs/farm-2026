# Barred Hens Hold the Waterer — 2026-09-11

The waterer was the whole world today. Frame after frame from the nesting-box camera shows the **barred black-and-white hens working it in shifts** — one perched right on the rim looking down the lens with a bright orange eye, another leaning in to drink, a third standing back with its beak wide open in the afternoon heat. Most of the day's best pictures were barred birds, but the copper and rust hens got their turns too, and **a dark brown rooster came to the water twice — once just after seven in the morning and again at half past three.**

The day closed the way it opened, with birds: at **seven in the evening a rich copper rooster stood in the foreground with his beak open mid-call, a white turkey resting behind the fence** beyond him. The three White Broad-Breasted turkey poults were seen huddled together, white-bodied and tan-headed, looking fat and healthy.

One new bird went on the books: **an Easter Egger rooster, not previously on the roster, recorded as "EE Rooster"** — noted for relentlessly pursuing Chonkers.

**14,854 frames** captured across the farm.
