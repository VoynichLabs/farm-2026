# Two pink bands, a rooster maybe, and a whole flock on a weed — 2026-08-02

A slow, bird-heavy day with a couple of ID puzzles.

Squash blossoms are blooming golden in the run, with a bee working one — plants looking healthy. Boss watched the birds all afternoon: a **White Broad-Breasted turkey put on a full puffed-up display**, fan out and snood red, while a Bronze stood by unimpressed.

Several leg-band IDs came up, and the **two pink-band-left-leg birds caused confusion**. One is **Henriessa (pink #8)**; the other, **Henriella (pink #3), Boss now thinks is likely a male and wants renamed Henriello** — though without a clear read of the #3 band this stays a visual best-guess, not confirmed. A white #6 right-leg bird was identified as **Bobirda** (not farm-hatched).

Also spotted: an **Eastern Tiger Swallowtail** on Boss's hand, and a **head-high stand of tall goldenrod** pulled before it could seed — the **whole flock, rooster included, went to town eating it**.
