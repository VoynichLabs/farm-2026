# The Rooster Held Court — 2026-09-03

The **red-comb rooster owned the day**. He turns up in seven of the twelve keeper frames, and every one of them is the same posture: head up, eye locked square on the lens, feathers crisp. Ten-forty by the water bowl. Three o'clock, twice in the same minute, once golden-brown in the light. Half past four with hens strung out behind him in a muddy yard. Half five, comb and wattle blazing. Whatever else moved through Birdcatraz today, he was standing in the middle of it.

The hens worked around him. A dark-feathered bird caught **mid-flap with both wings open** at 10:49, a white chicken alongside her. A **black-and-white speckled hen eating a leaf** at 14:29, beak in motion. Another bird **airborne mid-wingbeat** five minutes later. A speckled hen took the chair at 17:10 and held it. A **turkey drifted through the background** at four o'clock, off at the edge of the frame.

**20,421 frames** across the farm's cameras.

Off-camera, the pen finally got measured — **8 ft by 8.4 ft, 6.7 ft tall**, Producer's Pride steel and welded wire, with a 10x10 pink tarp already over the roof. A **20x10 tarp** is the call for wrapping two sides.
