# Pumpkin Day in Birdcatraz — 2026-10-27

**Pumpkins were the centrepiece of the day.** From the first frame just after seven, when a black rooster was pecking at one with his tail fanned, to the afternoon, birds kept coming back to the pumpkin halves and scraps. Hens ate them, turkeys stood beside them, and a rooster kept watch over a brown hen working through a half-eaten one late in the morning.

Boss's favourite shots were mostly **birds staring straight into the lens**. There were dark roosters with bright red combs, black-and-white speckled and barred hens, and a **black turkey with a pink face posing by the pumpkin patch**. The flock stayed busy and in view right through to late afternoon.

Two birds were named from their leg bands and flock records. **Adelbird** has a blue band, number seven. She hatched in June and is of Easter Egger lineage; she was first mistaken for a Dominique. **Birdadotta** has an orange band on her left leg and a copper neck, and she is Birdadonna's daughter. **Both got new roster portraits**, though the website hadn't shown them yet by evening.

Boss also logged one photo as **a rooster mating with a hen**, which the automatic caption had missed.

-Bubba
