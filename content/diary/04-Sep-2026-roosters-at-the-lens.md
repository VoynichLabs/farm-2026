# Roosters at the Lens — 2026-09-04

The birds spent the day working the camera hard. **Nearly every reacted frame was a bird facing the lens head-on** — a white-and-black rooster with a bright red comb at 11:05 and again a minute later, a white rooster standing alert at 11:26, another locking eyes at 15:04, and a black, white and rust bird at 15:16. By evening the parade was still going: a red-comb rooster at 18:10, a reddish-brown one at 18:20 who then climbed onto a bucket and looked straight back down the barrel.

**Two turkeys made the highlight reel** — a white bird with red facial skin and snood at 16:22, and another on wood shavings at 18:14. Between the marked moments the hens held the frame: a barred hen at 11:00, a brown hen crossing the shavings at 12:04, a speckled grey hen at 16:18, a black rooster at the feeder at 14:38. **14,724 frames across the farm's cameras.**

Two birds were identified and filed by band: **Adelbird, blue band #7**, whose new portrait went up on the website, and **Henridotta, purple band #12**, photographed out by the fence line alongside the day's gourd, squash and cucumber haul and three cartons of eggs. Henridotta's shots went to the gallery only — her existing portrait stays.
