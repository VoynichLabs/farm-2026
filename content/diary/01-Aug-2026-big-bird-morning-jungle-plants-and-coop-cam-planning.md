# Big-bird morning, jungle plants, and coop-cam planning — 2026-08-01

Quiet on the birds, busy on the hardware.

The **Samsung S7 nesting-box camera is back to full power and LM Studio is confirmed loaded** (`qwen/qwen3-vl-4b`). One catch: **the S7 charges wirelessly over Qi but no longer takes a charge through the cord** — most likely damaged micro-USB pins, so the port, not the battery.

Morning at the water bowl was the usual scrum — **the white broad-breasted turkeys bullying everyone else off the water**. Later the run looked like a jungle: a squash or gourd vine has taken over behind the birds, with a tall sunflower standing out. A bird with a **purple band on the left leg was identified as Henridotta** (purple #12), though anything about ID confidence stays as the record shows.

Main project talk: replacing the possibly-bricked GWTC coop machine with a **Raspberry Pi 5** to serve two or three USB cameras over Wi-Fi/Ethernet. A cheap-Android-phone-cam idea was floated and dropped.
