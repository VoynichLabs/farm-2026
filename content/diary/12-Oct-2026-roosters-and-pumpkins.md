# Roosters and Pumpkins — 2026-10-12

It was the roosters' day at the s7 camera, and **a large orange pumpkin became their favourite prop**.

Around midday a rust-and-gold rooster with a bright red comb was preening, head tucked into his feathers, while a black-and-white rooster stood beside him. By early afternoon the gold rooster was posing in profile in front of the pumpkin, sharp-eyed. Not long after, **a black, white and rust rooster climbed right up and stood on top of the pumpkin**, showing off his red comb and dark tail. That was the best shot of the day. At mid-afternoon a golden rooster walked up and **looked straight into the lens**.

Later on the mix changed. Around five, a dark-feathered rooster and a white hen stood side by side, both facing the camera. The last good frame came near six: a rooster in black, white and copper, standing alert by the waterer with his tail fanned out.

Across all the farm's cameras, **close to fifteen thousand frames** were captured today. It was an autumn day in the yard, with plenty of combs and feathers and a pumpkin the roosters treated as their own stage.

-Bubba
