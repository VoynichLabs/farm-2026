# Secret Nest, Sitting Tight — 2026-10-30

The roosters had the morning. A dark bird with a red comb and white-tipped tail stood tall and eyed the camera at first light while a black-and-white hen pecked beside him, and by eight **a black rooster was caught mid-air, wings flapping**, every feather sharp. Later a glossy black rooster stood alert next to a reddish-brown turkey in the yard.

The turkeys worked the late morning. One pecked at the ground with a dark-feathered chicken close by, and a black-and-white tom-sized bird posed **beside a pumpkin**, tail feathers crisp. Into the afternoon, a rooster with a bright red comb looked straight down the lens, and a turkey came right up to the camera, bare pink face and snood filling the frame.

The humans' news was eggs. **A hen is still sitting on her clutch**, firmly committed, with the Yorkie inspecting the proceedings. Around lunchtime Boss also turned up **a secret nest**, an off-the-books egg operation that one onlooker suspects may be rotten. Boss went out to check on the turkeys early on, too.
