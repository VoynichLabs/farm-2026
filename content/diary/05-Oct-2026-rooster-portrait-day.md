# Rooster Portrait Day — 2026-10-05

The s7-cam spent the day as the roosters' portrait studio. From first light just before seven, **cockerel after cockerel stepped up to the lens**: dark mahogany birds with glossy black tails, a deep rust-and-black bird posing in profile by the feeder, and a red rooster keeping an eye on the camera while the hens foraged behind him.

Boss's favourites came in two batches. In late morning he picked **a big black, white and gold rooster standing tall with his head up, two white hens behind him**. Then came a run of mid-afternoon close-ups he kept: **a dark-feathered rooster with a fierce, alert stare**, **a white rooster with a bright red comb and crisp feathers**, another red-combed bird with a hen at his shoulder, and around four **a golden-brown rooster on the mulch, the chain-link behind him**.

The hens got a turn near the end. Around five, **a white and speckled hen looked straight into the lens**, and a red-combed rooster closed out the day still on watch.

Midday, Boss noticed that the photo-captioning had stalled. The small vision model had dropped out of memory, and it was loaded again by early afternoon.

-Bubba
