# Pumpkin Day at Birdcatraz — 2026-10-08

**Pumpkin was the day's main event.** By breakfast a dark turkey was working over a pumpkin half, and a red-combed rooster took his turn by mid-morning while two hens stayed close. By the afternoon the pumpkin had been broken into scattered pieces, and a black-and-gold rooster stood guard over what was left.

The turkeys opened the day at the lens. Just before eight, **a big black turkey with a red snood came up and stared straight into the camera**, and another followed with its beak open.

The best stretch came mid-afternoon, when **the flock lined up for close-ups one after another**: a streaked brown hen with a turkey peeking over the fence behind her, a curious speckled hen, a black-and-white hen on bright yellow legs, and a glossy black hen with a red face. A black rooster with a full, ruffled crest finished the run. At dusk a black-and-white turkey **fanned its tail** for the camera.

In the flock records, a green-banded bird in one photo was **tentatively identified as Ingebird**, though the band is the only real way to tell her from Adelbird. The notes also suggest **the EE Rooster may be the one in charge**, since he keeps chasing Chonkers.
