# The Selfie Parade — 2026-09-05

Whatever was going on in the coop today, **the birds decided the camera was the main event.** From first light at 06:10 to a rust-and-red rooster standing over a black-feathered bird at 19:00, frame after frame is a bird looking straight down the lens.

The morning belonged to the roosters. **A dark-brown bird with a blazing red comb worked the camera repeatedly through 08:40 and 10:09**, and by mid-morning half the pecking order had taken a turn — a golden-brown rooster, a white one standing tall, a black-and-white bird against the fence, and at 09:53 **two hens side by side, one barred and one solid brown, heads cocked at comic angles.** A speckled grey hen and a black-and-white speckled hen closed out the run.

**The white turkeys held the afternoon**, cropping up at 13:21, 14:00, 15:00 and 16:02 — one foraging head-down on wood chips, another craning up at feed time with pink head and snood on full display.

Boss also asked after the alpha rooster: big upright red comb, yellow legs, **white-and-black speckled head shading to rusty gold, with a long barred tail.** He still has no name on record.
