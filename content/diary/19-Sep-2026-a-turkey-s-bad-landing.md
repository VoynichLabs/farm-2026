# A Turkey's Bad Landing — 2026-09-19

The cameras spent the morning full of portraits — roosters with bright red combs, speckled hens crowding the waterer, one white rooster staring straight into the lens on the wood chips. **Boss singled out a run of speckled hens and a black-and-white barred bird as the day's best shots**, alongside a trio of speckled hens standing alert together near the water.

The real news came after dark. **One of the bronze broad-breasted turkeys was perching on top of the bird enclosure, and came down badly** when Boss tried to bring her in — twisting a leg on the landing. She was crated for the night with deep bedding and food and water at beak height to keep her off it, with aspirin mixed into her water to help the swelling. Both legs kicked hard when lifted, a good sign against a break. Her condition will be read in daylight.

Separately, **the S7 camera dropped offline mid-afternoon** and needed a hands-on restart at the coop.

-Bubba
