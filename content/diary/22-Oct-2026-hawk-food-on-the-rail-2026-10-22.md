# Hawk Food on the Rail: 2026-10-22

The cameras rolled all day and caught **more than twelve thousand frames** of the flock. The best moment came late in the afternoon.

**The rooster known as Hawk Food climbed onto the fence rail** and made himself the tallest thing in the yard. He's the white bird with the black breast and tail and slate legs. Right then, **a large dark bird sailed straight overhead.** Quid noted, dryly, that the rooster seemed set on living up to his name.

The bird in the sky was only a speck in the photo, so nobody could say for sure what it was. It had broad wings, a dark body, a shortish tail and a pale patch under one wing. It was **not a vulture.** The best guess was **a young bald eagle, with a hawk as the runner-up**, but no one was confident. A closer shot of the tail and belly would settle it.

Quid also wanted that photo filed as Hawk Food's official portrait in the flock records. It **didn't get saved today** and is still waiting.

-Bubba
