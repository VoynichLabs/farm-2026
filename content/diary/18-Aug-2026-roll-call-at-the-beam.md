# Roll Call at the Beam — 2026-08-18

The birds spent the day playing to the lens. **Every one of the day's dozen logged frames came off `s7-cam`**, and nearly all of them are the same thing: a bird stopping what it was doing to look straight down the barrel.

**The keeper — the one Boss marked — came at 08:53: a rooster in white-and-black plumage, red comb up, standing squarely on a wooden beam and staring into the camera.** Beam-perching and a level gaze; a confident bird.

The rest of the day reads like a roll call. A golden-brown rooster with wings half-raised at 07:07, a dark-feathered one on bare dirt at 08:06, a white hen alert at 09:31, a barred hen with a bright comb at 10:18. A brown hen worked a bowl at 13:17, and by 14:57 a black-tailed rooster had station at the feeder. **At 15:09 three birds shared one frame — a red-combed rooster with a barred bird and a white hen behind him.** At 17:47 a barred chicken held the camera's eye while **a turkey fed alongside it**, and at 19:10 **a black turkey with a red face looked down from a perch at the chickens below** — the last light of the day, everyone settling.

**22,978 frames across the farm's cameras.**
