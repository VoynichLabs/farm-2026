# Buff Ranger Skin Irritation — "Buff Buttrot" — 2026-06-10

One of the **Buff Rangers** (the May TSC batch juveniles) has an irritated patch of skin on the flank, under the wing — raw and reddened with feathers missing around it (`IMG_5499`).

**Boss's diagnosis:** a skin condition / irritated rash — **not** a bird-on-bird wound and **not** turkey aggression. Boss has called it **"buff buttrot"** and is administering the appropriate bird first aid himself.

> Bubba's initial read flagged possible feather-pecking or turkey trauma — given the run crowding and the documented bronze/white turkey aggression (white BB turkey poult attacked Boss on 18-May). Boss inspected the bird in hand and overruled that: it presents as skin irritation, not a trauma wound.

Logged for the record. Bird is under treatment. Things to watch as it heals: whether the patch spreads, signs of infection (swelling, pus, smell), and — given the warm June weather — flystrike around the raw skin.
