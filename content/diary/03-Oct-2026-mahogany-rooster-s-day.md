# Mahogany Rooster's Day — 2026-10-03

The cameras found the flock busy from first light to evening. Two photos stood out as the day's keepers: at dawn, **a grey rooster with a rust-and-white cape standing alert while the hens foraged behind him**, and mid-morning, **a rust-and-gold hen in close profile, every fine feather in focus.**

**A mahogany rooster held the frame for most of the day.** He showed up again and again from late morning into the evening, comb bright red, glossy black tail, picking his way across the mulch. Food was the main event. A brown rooster pecked at a scrap of bread early on, a white-and-black speckled hen leaned right up to the lens over some corn around midday, and orange feed lay scattered by late afternoon. Barred hens foraged in the sun, and in the afternoon **a speckled turkey worked the same pen** next to the rooster and a rust hen.

Birdimir got a fresh portrait. The speckled young bird with the red leg band was photographed on the blue tarp, and **the new photo now heads her page on the farm site**. Since she hatched here, her hatch page got the new picture too.
