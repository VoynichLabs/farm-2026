# The turkey pen meets the bulldog, and a sparrow crashes the coop — 2026-08-04

Two grounded farm threads today.

**MegaBird walked the boarding English bulldog, leashed, up to all five turkeys for a first meeting** — three white broad-breasteds and both bronzes. Reported as a wary standoff: **the turkeys aren't sure about him yet**, nobody bolted, nobody blinked. Earlier the birds had been locked in for the night and the dog worn out.

On equipment: the plan to offload the coop cameras onto a **Raspberry Pi 5 firmed up around the 2GB board over the 4GB.** The Pi is only a dumb capture node — grab frames from the cameras and push them to the Mac Mini over Ethernet — so 2GB has ample headroom; 4GB only earns its keep if detection ever runs on the Pi itself, which it won't.

A **tiny sparrow** turned up perched on the coop fence wire in a photo, so small the person who took it couldn't spot it — **likely a Chipping Sparrow**, though a Song Sparrow wasn't ruled out. Possibly drawn by scattered grain or the yard's sunflowers.
