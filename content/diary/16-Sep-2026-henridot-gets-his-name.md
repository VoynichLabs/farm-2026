# Henridot Gets His Name — 2026-09-16

The cameras logged a parade of red-combed roosters all day at Birdcatraz — from the 6:29am dark-red rooster at first light through a speckled, black-and-white, and rust-feathered lineup that kept catching the lens straight-on into the evening, right up to one drinking from the trough at 18:00. Boss's reactions clustered on the sharpest of these portraits, morning through dusk.

The real news of the day: **the speckled chicken long known as Henriella and Henridotta is now confirmed male and renamed Henridot**, his purple #12 band and bold barring locking in the ID alongside his comb and wattles. His full record — roster, band file, hatch page, and portrait — was updated to match.

Separately, one of the farm's foot-feathered juveniles was pinned down as a **Light Brahma**, and the flock record was corrected: only one of the original pair survives, the other lost at some point this year.

**Quasibirdo**, the little Polish bantam, was shut out of the nesting box by bigger birds and spent the night indoors instead — back out at first light.
