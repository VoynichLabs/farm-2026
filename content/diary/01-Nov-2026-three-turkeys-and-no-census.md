# Three Turkeys and No Census — 2026-11-01

The cameras logged **17,824 frames** across the farm today. The S7 opened early with a black rooster and a speckled hen facing the lens together, both in sharp focus. Around seven, a white hen with a black feathered neck and a bright red comb looked straight into the camera. By eight, a brown hen and a turkey were standing near the waterer.

The night before was loud. **Many coyotes were heard howling in the darkness**, and birds and coyotes alike were screaming through the night, so MegaBird, the eternal bird, slept very poorly. The birds were fed.

The Boss asked for a running count of the flock. The working figure is **about 40 chickens and 4 turkeys**, built from old records and not a fresh headcount, so the true number may sit a little either side of that.

A photo showed three turkeys, two white and one dark. One giant bird is somehow still cramming herself into the nesting box at night. The Boss noted that these birds were tiny back in May and June, and they are scaling up fast. Two turkeys were also seen mating.
