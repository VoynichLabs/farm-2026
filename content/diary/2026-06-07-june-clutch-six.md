# The June Clutch — All Six — 2026-06-07

The complete June (NI) clutch of six healthy hatchlings, together in the brooder bin.

**Photo:** `public/photos/june-2026/june-clutch-six-IMG_5307.jpg`

**What's in frame (IMG_5307):**
- Six chicks on pine shavings in the blue brooder bin, plus one unhatched marked egg in the foreground.
- Five are dark gray/black down; one (far right) is pale yellow — that is **Birdimir**, the only light-down chick of the clutch (black head spot).
- The five dark chicks — Ingebird, Henriessa, Horstabird, Henridotta, Adelbird — are near-identical at this age and are deliberately **not** individually labeled in this group shot.

The six birds are Birdimir, Ingebird, Henriessa, Horstabird, Henridotta, and Adelbird — the last hatches of the 2026 season. Per-bird detail (lineage, tells, photos) lives in `content/hatches/2026/`.
