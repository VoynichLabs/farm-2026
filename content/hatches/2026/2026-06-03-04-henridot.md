# Henridot — June (NI) clutch, named for his mother Henrietta

> **Renamed 16-Sep-2026:** hatched and recorded as **Henridotta**; the Boss confirmed him a cockerel in #meet-the-lobsters on 2026-09-16 ("Henridot as he will be known now") and he is **Henridot** from that date. Observations, captions and photo filenames dated before 16-Sep-2026 use the hatch name — they are append-only and were not rewritten.

Egg #7 of the June clutch — **Henrietta's own brown egg, marked 5/10** — hatched
~18:22 EDT on 2026-06-03. Named in honor of her mother, **Henrietta**, the
matriarch Golden Laced Wyandotte who founded the Henrietta line and was in her
final days as the clutch hatched. Sire window Whitey Red Legs per the NI-clutch
paternity record.

**Silver — resolved 2026-07-21: NOT Henridotta.** The Boss briefly assigned
"the silver one" to Henridotta on 2026-06-04 (flip-flopping from an earlier call
on Henriessa, phrased as a question), but on 2026-07-21 he confirmed visually
that the silver/blue-gray bird is **Henriessa**. Henridotta is the darker
standard look — the same coloring as **Adelbird** (slightly smaller). The
silver wing-tip/stripe marking is a Henrietta-line family tell, which is why the
two brown-egg sisters were hard to tell apart until now. First solo portrait
committed 2026-07-21 (IMG_7713) with **purple leg band #12** clearly visible —
the band closes the ID gap permanently.

**Sex — CONFIRMED COCKEREL (Boss-confirmed 16-Sep-2026, ~15 weeks).** Stated directly by the Boss in #meet-the-lobsters: *"Henridot as he will be known now."* This record carried no prior sex prediction — the hatch-day entry read `sex: unknown` and made no comb/wattle call — so the confirmation rests on the Boss's field call alone, not on any plumage inference. Renamed **Henridotta → Henridot** on this confirmation; "Henridotta" is retained as `formerly` (which drives the "fka" chip and the 308 from the old `/flock/henridotta` URL) and as `alternative_name`.
