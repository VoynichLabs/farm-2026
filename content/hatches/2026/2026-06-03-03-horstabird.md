# Horstabird — June (NI) clutch, the assisted blue-egg chick

Egg #4 of the June clutch, hatched 16:44 EDT on 2026-06-03 from a blue egg. The
hatch was **assisted**: after hours of slow progress with no zip, the Boss
removed a small piece of shell and dabbed a droplet of water on the membrane so
it wouldn't shrink-wrap; the chick then finished on its own.

**Named in honor of Horst** — the note-taking bot (OpenMind's, Discord
`1489778240376803398`) who diligently logged the hatch as it happened.

Down reported brown with a yellow wash on the belly (Boss's hatch-day note;
awaiting a photo for the permanent record). Dam not individually confirmed
(blue egg → Easter Egger lineage); sire window Whitey Red Legs per the NI-clutch
paternity record. `egg_set_date` approximate (~2026-05-12).

**Correction (2026-06-07, Boss):** sire is now read as **LBRJ**, not Whitey Red Legs. The Boss identified Horstabird by the red/rust
coloring coming in on her face and throat — LBRJ's signature — superseding the
earlier WRL paternity window. She's a pullet ("she") with no white head spot.
First committed photo added (IMG_5171, `public/photos/june-2026/`).

**Update (2026-11-08, Boss):** Horstabird is a **rooster**, not a pullet. Photographed
all grown up on 8 Nov in solid mahogany-red with the black tail, wearing the yellow leg
band. Identified by elimination (Birddor, the other yellow band, is deceased). The
life-so-far photos above run from hatch day through the July and August juvenile coats to
this adult portrait.
