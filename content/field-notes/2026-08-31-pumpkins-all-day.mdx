The birds spent the entire day on **cut pumpkins and squash**, and the camera barely caught a frame without one in it. From first light at 06:13 — a white rooster standing over a pumpkin while a black rooster pecked beside him — to the last look at 19:00, the pumpkins were the center of flock life.

**Boss kept fourteen frames today, more than any recent day, and nearly all of them are birds walking right up to the lens.** A barred hen eyeing the camera at 06:44 over a pumpkin. A dark rooster with a bright red comb feeding at 07:30. A white rooster standing alert at 07:32. The turkeys came through mid-morning: **a red-snooded tom staring straight down the barrel at 08:43** with a small brown chicken and a second turkey feeding nearby, and another dark turkey beside a pumpkin at 09:12.

The busiest stretch was 09:00–11:00 — roosters, hens and turkeys sharing the same pumpkin in mixed groups. A copper-and-gold rooster held the lens twice in a row at 10:40 and 10:41.

**24,093 frames captured across the farm's cameras today**, all six online.
