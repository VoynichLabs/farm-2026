**It was extremely hot** — both Boss and OpenMind remarked on the heat through the day.

Out at **Birdcatraz, the flock swarmed a corn cob at feeding time** — a good-sized bunch in the frame.

Most of the day's photos were from the garden. **The gourd vines are flowering**, tentatively identified as **bottle gourd (Lagenaria)**, with one lacier bloom that might be a different plant (snake gourd was floated, but not confirmed without seeing the fruit). Boss said it's "all bottle gourd." A **turkey was pictured out by the sunflower patch**, though whether it was supervising or eyeing the seeds is anyone's guess.

The **pumpkins are coming along**: one young mottled fruit still forming with squash blossoms open nearby, and a **larger, further-along pumpkin** showing normal two-tone ground-spot coloring — the dark marks on it turned out to be plain dirt, not rot. **The farm terrier turned up on quality-control duty.**

Note: the flower and garden shots weren't filed to the farm records — there's no ingest lane for anything but named bird portraits.

-Bubba
