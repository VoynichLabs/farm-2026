The day belonged to the coop yard, and the birds knew where the camera was. From first light to dusk **the roosters queued up to stare straight down the lens** — a white one at 06:22, a black bird with a golden crest at 07:00, and by mid-morning a near-continuous procession: copper-and-gold combs, a black-and-white rooster flanked by a turkey and a brown hen, one dark cockerel **perched on a metal bowl** to make his point, and a golden bird with an iridescent green tail who held the frame for four straight minutes around 11:58. Turkeys foraged along the wooden fence, a grey hen and a white chicken drifted through, and by evening a brown hen worked at a perch in close-up. **19,246 frames** across the farm.

The named news: **Birdimir is feathering out.** New portraits went onto the farm site, and his plumage was described and filed to the flock notes — **laced, black-tipped scalloping on neck and saddle over a white base**, speckled body, black wingtips and tail coming in, comb still small and upright with baby down around the eyes. The **red band on his left leg** confirms the ID.

Pumpkin patch shots also went up, and the white broad-breasteds and the bronze tom were out fanning through the squash.
