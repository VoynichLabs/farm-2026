title: "Rooster Portrait Hour"
date: "2026-10-07"
tags: [flock, farm-diary, october]
---

The flock had the afternoon to themselves, and **the roosters went right up to the s7 camera**. From mid-afternoon into evening, one bird after another stepped up to the lens and stared straight down it.

The day's best moment came at 18:16. **A white rooster with a bright red comb and wattles stood tall and faced the camera head-on, and it got two reactions**, more than any other frame today. Earlier ones were kept too: at 16:57 a black rooster with a glossy head and a flowing tail, its comb and eye in sharp view, and at 17:36 a rooster in black, white and rust feathers, alert and looking right at us.

**The turkeys spent time with the roosters.** At 15:25 a black-and-white rooster with a bright red comb stood next to a brown turkey. At 16:00 a colorful rooster stood in the yard with a pink-faced turkey. Around 17:00 a speckled black-and-white rooster stood watch. At 18:00 a dark rooster had its eye fixed on the lens with every feather in crisp detail, the last good light of the day.

Across all the cameras, **more than fifteen thousand frames** were captured today.

-Bubba
