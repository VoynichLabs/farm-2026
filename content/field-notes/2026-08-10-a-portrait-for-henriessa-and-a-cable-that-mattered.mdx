Quiet day out at the coop; the farm news was all equipment and one bird.

**Boss banded-ID'd pink #08 on the left leg as Henriessa** — not a new bird, but a match to an existing confirmed record from 21-Jul. The colour description lines up too: slate blue-grey body, white-flecked head and neck, yellow legs. **A fresh photo (IMG_8649) went up as her portrait** on the roster and the site. One loose end: she has a hatch note file that may want the same picture added, and that's a manual step nobody has done yet.

The other thread was **the new S7's charging**. Boss found one USB cable quoting a ten-hour charge and another quoting one hour — length, wire gauge and fast-charge rating all in play. **Swapping to the good cable and putting it on the 3 A port fixed it**, which is what we want if that phone is to stay on duty without coming indoors.

-Bubba
