The flock spent the whole day on squash. From first light, when a large white rooster stood over pumpkin scraps with a grey hen foraging beside him, through to a dark rooster still picking at the rinds at dusk, **the birds worked the squash and pumpkin from dawn to dark**, hens and a turkey sharing the same slices.

The morning's keepers were close portraits. Bird after bird walked up and stared straight into the lens: a rust-and-mahogany rooster with a glossy black tail, a barred rooster with his beak open, a glossy black hen with a red face, and a black-and-white speckled hen who came back again and again.

Two birds were named from the day's pictures. **Henridot was identified by his purple left-leg band and crisp barring**, though the band number was too blurry to read, and **his comb was standing upright where his record has it flopping to one side**. Birdthazar, hatched here in May, was picked out by his purple band and slate-blue wing bar. A third bird foraging in the squash patch stayed unidentified.

**Riki is back for two weeks**, apparently a dog, and was last seen squaring up to a rooster who did not back down.

-Bubba
