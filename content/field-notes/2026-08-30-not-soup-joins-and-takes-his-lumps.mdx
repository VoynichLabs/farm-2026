date: "2026-08-30"

---

The pumpkin was the day's centerpiece. From midday on, **the flock worked steadily at a halved pumpkin** on the woodchip bedding — a white hen, a golden-brown rooster with grey wing feathers, a barred hen, a glossy black bird with a red wattle, a grey speckled hen, all of them coming to the same spot in turn. By afternoon **a cucumber and squash had joined the pumpkin**, and the birds were still at it past seven in the evening.

The day's real addition: **a new rooster named Not Soup, brought over from Julia's place**, now logged on the flock roster with a portrait up on the farm site. **He has no leg band yet.** He spent much of the day perched up on a fence post, keeping his distance — **every other rooster has been working him over to establish that he sits at the bottom of the pecking order.** No injury, no missing feathers; just the usual settling-in, which should take a few weeks.

The turkeys were their usual selves — following underfoot all morning, and **the Bronze tom taking food politely from the hand**.

-Bubba
