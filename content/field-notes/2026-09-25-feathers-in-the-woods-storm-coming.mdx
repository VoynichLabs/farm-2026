The S7 camera logged a full day of flock portraits, and Boss reacted to a dozen. Roosters and hens kept walking up to the lens. There was **a reddish-brown rooster with a bright red comb** and **a black-and-gold rooster flanked by rust-colored hens**. A black-and-white speckled rooster stared straight in, and so did a glossy black hen and a grey-and-black hen with a white crest. Another rooster was caught drinking from a bowl. By evening a white rooster with a flowing tail stood tall for the camera, and turkeys watched from the background and behind the fence.

The sad news is that **the female white turkey is gone.** Boss found a large scatter of white feathers in the woods behind the pen but no carcass. The flock record now lists her as lost, presumed taken in broad daylight, and **two white turkeys remain.** One camera was turned around to face where the feathers lay.

**A wind and rain storm is on the way**, with a wind advisory through Sunday morning and gusts near fifty miles an hour. It's worth tying down loose gear, checking tarps and camera mounts, and expecting a power flicker. The Pi running the coop cameras has no battery backup, so it would drop first.

-Bubba
