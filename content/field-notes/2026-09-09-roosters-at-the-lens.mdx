date: "2026-09-09"

From first light to dusk the birds worked the camera. **Nearly every frame the day produced has a chicken staring straight down the lens** — a black rooster at the fence at 06:22, a white one alert at 07:01, a speckled hen at 08:00, and on through the afternoon: reddish-brown, black-and-white, brown, one last white bird at 18:05. **22,726 frames** came in across the farm's cameras. Two portraits were kept as the day's best: **a reddish-brown rooster at 11:16 and a white rooster at 13:08**, both combs bright red, both looking dead ahead.

Morning brought the flock piling up on the waterer with a turkey angling for scraps; by late afternoon the whole group was free-ranging and picking through the clippings.

On the records side, the roster now stands at **11 active roosters**, with **Chonkers and Birdthazar still logged only as "probable cockerel."** **Malt Liquor was confirmed by his Black-Breasted Red Old English Game coloring**, and **Quasibirdo's portrait went live on the site.**

In the garden, **powdery mildew on the squash leaves and Choanephora blossom rot in the fruit** — both fed by dew that never burns off in the shade.

-Bubba
