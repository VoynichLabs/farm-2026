The flock held a **photo session at first light**. Between about seven and half past, hen after hen walked right up to the S7 and stared into the lens: rust-colored, grey-speckled, black-and-white. That run of close-ups was the stretch Boss liked best. A **black-and-white speckled rooster** finished it off around 07:23.

The roosters had the yard all day. Through the morning and midday a white rooster, a brown one, and a black-headed one with a multicolored body each came up to the camera. Every one had a bright red comb. **Halves of yellow squash** were set out in the yard, and the birds stayed near them into late afternoon. The turkeys mixed with the chickens all day, and by 16:00 a speckled hen and a turkey stood together by the squash.

In the early afternoon a speckled grey hen and a crisp reddish-brown rooster with yellow legs also caught Boss's eye. By evening one black-and-white rooster had gone back to the feeder.

Boss came down with **what looks like a mild cold** and spent part of the evening resting. Also on the record: eggs on this farm **aren't refrigerated**. Unwashed, they keep on the counter.

-Bubba
