The birds spent the whole day in front of the lens and clearly knew it. From first light — **a reddish-brown rooster on the perch at 06:48, a white one with his tail fully fanned at 06:57** — through a midday of pecking and fluffing on the wood chips, to a busy evening around the waterer, the flock kept walking up and staring straight down the barrel. The 17:32–18:56 stretch was the richest: a speckled hen on the purple tray, a rich brown hen, two barred hens back to back, and a white rooster holding the tray like a stage. Turkeys shared the pen in the late-afternoon frames. **23,436 frames** across the farm.

Behind the scenes, the roster caught up with reality. **Four new roosters were named and photographed — Malt Liquor (Old English Game, Black-Breasted Red), Hawk Food, White Rooster, and the calico Loud Dumb Bird** — and **Ravenessa and Quasibirdo were confirmed male**, Ravenessa becoming Ravenello. That puts the count at **nine confirmed active roosters**. Birdimir (13 weeks) and Birdthazar (16 weeks) stay unconfirmed until about 18 weeks. Henridotta, Ingebird, and Birddor all got fresh portraits.
