Build spec

Run (Version A): full X cross-bracing, bottom rail at ground; 2″×4″ welded wire (72″ × 150 ft roll). No full buried wire-mesh ground — too costly for this build. The ½″ hardware-cloth bottom band + buried L-apron is an optional add, done only where digging actually shows up and budget allows.

Roof (the priority): 2″×4″ welded wire over the cross-bars, positively fastened with j-clips/hog rings at every frame member (wind uplift peels resting-contact mesh); short spans (4–6 ft) between uprights and a slight pitch so snow sheds instead of loading the span; corner-post footings carry the snow weight. Built as removable/openable framed sections (split the 14×14 ft footprint into 2–3), framed in EMT conduit or aluminum angle (not steel). Rule: a section is open only while someone is standing under it.

Garden (~120 ft perimeter, one 150 ft roll): 2″ hex netting; fold the bottom 6–8″ outward and pin for rabbits/groundhogs. For deer: a two-strand high line (~2 ft and ~5 ft, 18″ apart), monofilament or bailing wire; flag it with tape the first season.

Purple zone: strategic reserve — hold for coop/run expansion if adding birds, otherwise extend the garden into it.

Observed predators: coyotes, bobcats, hawks, possibly raccoons — no foxes so far. Climbers/jumpers and aerial, so the roof is the real defense; the buried anti-dig apron stays optional until digging is actually seen.

Sourcing: Tractor Supply or Home Depot. Not eBay (shipping). Amazon only with free Prime shipping and a good bulk price.
